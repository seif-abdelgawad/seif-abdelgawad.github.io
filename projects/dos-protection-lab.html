Simulated a TCP SYN Flood attack against Sophos XG in an isolated VMware lab using hping3, generating high-volume flood traffic to stress-test the firewall's DoS protection.

Method

  • Generated high-volume TCP SYN flood traffic against Sophos XG using hping3 from an isolated VMware host.
  • Captured baseline behavior with DoS protection disabled, then re-ran the same attack with the policy enabled.

Results

  • Attack traffic passed through freely without DoS protection enabled.
  • 100% of flood packets were blocked after enabling the DoS protection policy.
  • Reviewed firewall security logs to confirm full detection and mitigation, producing a structured before/after analysis report.
